# Reference data

**sport_days.csv** (87 rows): England men's football at Euro 2020 (7), World Cup 2022 (5), Euro 2024 (7) and World Cup 2026 (8), plus every Six Nations match involving England or Ireland 2021-2026 (10 rows a year; England v Ireland appears twice, once per team). Range 2021-01-01 to 2026-10-31. `date` and `kickoff_local` are UK local (Europe/London). Football comes from [openfootball](https://github.com/openfootball) (CC0-1.0, no attribution needed). Its times are venue-local with no offset for Euro 2020/2024 (CEST) and WC2022 (Qatar, UTC+3), so the script converts them with those fixed offsets; WC2026 times carry their own UTC offset, and England v Mexico (5 Jul, 18:00 UTC-6) becomes 2026-07-06 01:00 UK. Six Nations dates and kick-offs come from the wikitext of the English Wikipedia articles `{year}_Six_Nations_Championship` via the MediaWiki API (facts from Wikipedia contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0; attribution: "Wikipedia contributors", with the article URL in `source_url`). Produced by `python3 scripts/fetch_reference.py`; raw downloads are cached in `raw/` (delete a file to re-fetch).

**duty_changes.csv** (4 rows): UK beer duty changes affecting a pub, 2021-2026. Rates are £ per litre of pure alcohol for beer at 3.5-8.4% ABV. The 2021-01-01 row stands for the old-system freeze (£19.08 per hl per % ABV, which has no per-litre-of-alcohol equivalent, so the rate is blank). The 1 Feb 2024 freeze extension (no rate change) and the announced 1 Feb 2027 RPI rise (4.55%) are left out. Written by hand from the HMRC "Historic Alcohol Duty rates" table (https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/alcohol-bulletin/alcohol-bulletin-historic-duty-rates), the Finance (No. 2) Act 2023 Part 2 and HMRC tax information notes, cited per row. These are UK public-sector information under the Open Government Licence v3.0.
